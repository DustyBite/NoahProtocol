Object manipulation is an important part of this simulation. To pick up an object:
- look at the object till it the outline appears.
- Press and hold [left mouse button]
- Drag object around.
- pressing and holding [Right mouse button] will reset the objects orientation.

title: PSU CASSETTE
image: res://DevAssets/Materials/PalletOne/Textures/DT_Green.png
unlock: start
---

The PSU Cassette cards are the main objects you will be processing
- The front indicator light (Non blinking light) shows the status of the card.
Green: Ready to be processed
Orange: Mid processing
Blue: Fully processed
Red: Broken, unable to be processed.
